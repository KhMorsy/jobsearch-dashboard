Privacy Policy — Job Search Agents

Last updated: 2026-10-10

Job Search Agents ("the app") is a personal automation tool operated by Khaled Morsy for his own job search. It is not offered to the public and is used by a single person.

What data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): reads the operator's own incoming email to identify job applications, responses, interview invitations, and job-alert messages.
  • Gmail (send): sends job-shortlist digest emails to the operator's own address.

How data is used and stored

Extracted information (application status, job listings, learning progress) is stored in the operator's own private GitHub repository and is used only to produce a personal dashboard and Telegram notifications. The app does not sell, share, or transmit this data to any third party. Email contents are processed transiently to classify messages and are not published.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Gemini for text classification, the Telegram Bot API for notifications, and public job-search APIs. Only the minimum necessary text is sent to these services to perform their function.

Retention and revocation

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data lives in the operator's private repository and can be deleted by the operator at any time.
